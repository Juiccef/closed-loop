## How Closed Loop speaks to these numbers

- **Living alone with memory changes** is exactly where "did you do it?" fails: a hedged "I think so" is the honest answer, and a reminder that hears "yes" learns nothing. Closed Loop records it as *said they would, not sure* and asks again.
- **Unpaid family caregivers** carry the follow-up. Closed Loop emails them only when a check-in stays open, caps emails per day, and lets one click stop the follow-ups once they have been in touch.
- **Family who live elsewhere** can ask their own Alexa how the day went and hear exactly what was recorded; their assistant can never answer for the person.
- **After Alexa Together**, Amazon's page points remote caregivers to the Alexa Emergency Assist subscription, which is built around emergency help. Closed Loop is deliberately not an emergency service; it covers the ordinary days in between.

## What these numbers do not show

They describe the size of the need, not evidence that Closed Loop helps. There are no users, pilots, or outcomes yet, and the submission does not claim any.
