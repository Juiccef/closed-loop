Closed Loop

A daily check-in for someone who lives alone. It records what they said honestly, follows up later, and tells family only when the loop stays open.

Example
Good morning, Margaret. Have you done your morning routine yet?
I think so, maybe after lunch
Okay. I'll ask again around 1 pm.
Recorded: said later

Before you try it

  • Simulated Alexa+ client. Not Amazon software. Alexa+ add-on tooling is partner-only, so this project implements the MCP server side and demonstrates it through a self-built MCP client, as the hackathon FAQ allows.
  • Reminders and check-ins only. Not an emergency alert system. No answer does not mean an emergency.
  • Check-in conversations are started by the person, a household routine, or the simulator. MCP add-ons have no documented way to speak first.
